Privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026.

The app

Dictora turns speech into text using Apple’s on-device speech recognition and Apple’s on-device language model. Dictora has no account system and no server of its own.

This website

This website uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count visits and see which parts of the page people use: page views, clicks on download buttons, which writing style is tried in the demo, FAQ questions opened and how far down the page visitors scroll. Analytics may set cookies or similar identifiers and is processed by Google.

  • It is not loaded if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.
  • You can turn it off at any time with “Turn off analytics” in the footer.
  • We do not collect your name or email on this site, and there are no forms.

Downloads

Downloading the app is a plain file download from this site’s hosting provider, Firebase Hosting, which keeps standard server logs.
